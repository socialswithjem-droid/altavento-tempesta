01STORY

BORN
FROM WIND.

Altavento means high wind. Tempesta is what happens when that wind meets the road.

Drawn in Modena as a road car with the discipline of a track machine, the Tempesta exists in a single series of twenty.

Every surface answers to the air around it. Every part is weighed, questioned and refined until nothing is left that does not earn its place.
